Hidavo Terms of Use

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Hidavo is a personal quiz. You name people you know and answer “who among these” questions. It is not a live social network and does not require those people to join.

Your use

You must be at least 14. Use the app only for fun, personal quizzes. Do not harass anyone. Do not enter other people’s private data.

Content

Answers stay on your device in this version. You are responsible for the names you type.

No account on our servers

This version does not create a Hidavo server account. Deleting the app data or using Delete account removes the local quiz from this device.
