Hidavo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Hidavo is a personal “who among these people” quiz. You type a login, your display name, and first names of people you know. You then answer questions about that group. Those people do not need an account, and the app does not message them.

What we store on your device

This version keeps data only on your device: your login, display name, optional university and year, the first names you typed, your star balance, and quiz progress. There is no Hidavo server account in this build.

What we do not collect

We do not read your contacts, photos, or phone number. We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads in this version.

Names you type

First names you enter are answer labels for your quiz. Enter only names of people you know. Do not enter phone numbers, full legal names, or other private data.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the system share sheet can send a link to Hidavo. We do not upload your quiz answers when you share.

Delete your data

Settings → Delete account wipes the local data immediately. That cannot be undone.

Age

Hidavo is for people 14 and older.
