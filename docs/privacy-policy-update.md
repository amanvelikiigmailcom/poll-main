**Effective date: November 22, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how TOO Hidavo, Kazakhstan (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information in Flyprox, on our information pages and when you contact support.

## 1. Versions and your choices

Flyprox offers a name-based game with optional phone sign-in and online features in version 1.0.2 and later, including TestFlight releases. Earlier versions may offer only local play. The processing below depends on the version and features you use.

You can play as a guest with nicknames, fictional names or examples. Phone sign-in, selecting contacts, profile images, notifications, external sharing and Premium are optional. Declining notifications or not selecting contacts does not prevent guest play.

## 2. Information on your device

The app stores your nickname, participant labels, optional profile fields such as university/year, local profile image, star balance, answers, round progress, question history, timer and settings. Downloaded questions and screen text may be cached for offline use.

Guest answers and manually entered participant labels stay on your device. Your local profile image is selected through the system image picker; the current local profile flow does not upload it to our backend. Local data may be included in operating-system backups depending on your settings.

## 3. Phone accounts and online rounds

If you choose phone sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes your phone number, verification information and technical information needed to authenticate you and prevent abuse. Google may use phone numbers for abuse prevention across Google services. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/).

Our backend stores your account phone number and account/session identifiers to operate sign-in. Signed-in online rounds store question identifiers, round status and votes linked to account identifiers, including the recipient where a selected contact matches a registered account. Local-only or skipped choices are not sent as votes for another person. Do not assume that online votes are anonymous to the operator.

## 4. Contacts you choose

Tapping “Choose from contacts” opens Apple's contact picker. Flyprox receives only the entries you select, not a background copy of your entire address book.

Selected names are used as labels on your quiz cards. If signed in, the app normalizes selected phone numbers on your device and sends hashes of those numbers and the selected labels to our backend to find registered friends. Raw selected-contact phone numbers and your full address book are not uploaded. Hashes can be matched to accounts and are not anonymous data.

Selecting a contact does not send an invitation. A matched registered friend may receive a notification when you cast an online vote for them, if they have enabled notifications. Use nicknames where possible and avoid sensitive information.

## 5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app sends a device push token and device/account identifiers to our backend and Appwrite Messaging so Apple Push Notification service can deliver notifications. Notification text does not name the voter. Delivery depends on permissions, device settings and network availability; it is not guaranteed.

You can decline the notification request and change your preference in iOS Settings.

## 6. Premium, purchases and interaction information

Flyprox offers an optional auto-renewing Premium Weekly subscription. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your payment-card details. The price and terms shown in the purchase screen and Apple's confirmation sheet apply.

The app uses Superwall to operate purchases, restore access and understand purchase flows. Its SDK processes purchase/subscription information, installation or app-user identifiers, device information and product interaction events, such as app launches, paywall views and purchase actions, for functionality and analytics. Technical information can include IP-derived region. See [Superwall privacy information](https://superwall.com/privacy).

We do not sell your participant names or game choices, display third-party advertising, or use these features to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

## 7. Sharing and support

WhatsApp sharing opens prepared invitation text. Instagram sharing attempts to open Instagram's recipient picker with a greeting. A separate Copy action uses the clipboard. These actions do not include your participant list or answers. You decide whether to send a message. The timer shortcut is granted for tapping the relevant sharing button; we do not verify delivery or another person's registration.

If you email us, report a question or submit the support form, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, question text and attachments. Reports prepared by the app exclude participant names and answers. Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment details or private participant information.

## 8. Services and international processing

We use a self-hosted backend and Appwrite for accounts, game data and messaging, Cloudflare for API connectivity, Firebase for authentication, Apple for distribution, payments and push delivery, and Superwall for subscription functionality and analytics. Our information pages are hosted by Notion and the optional support form is provided by Tally. These services may process technical information or information you provide in countries other than your own under their applicable terms.

Provider information: [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/), [Superwall](https://superwall.com/privacy), [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), [Notion](https://www.notion.com/privacy), [Tally](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy). Email providers process support correspondence.

## 9. Retention, reset and deletion requests

We retain account and online game information while needed to provide the features you use, handle support, prevent abuse and meet applicable obligations. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address requests and related recordkeeping. Different providers have their own retention rules.

“Reset game” removes local game data and, when signed in and connected, attempts to request deletion of the Flyprox backend account data. A local reset alone is not proof that all server or provider records have been erased, particularly if the network request fails. Contact [support@flyprox.com](mailto:support@flyprox.com) to request account/provider-data deletion or confirmation; do not send a password or verification code. We may need to verify account ownership.

Resetting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription, delete messages in external apps or remove device backups. Manage subscriptions in Apple Account settings and backups through your device settings.

## 10. Age, rights and contact

Flyprox is intended for people aged 13 and over, subject to higher local age or parental-permission requirements. It is not intended for children under 13. The Premium age check is not a guarantee that every user has been independently age-verified.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control personal information. Contact us for assistance. You can also use guest play, avoid optional data, decline notifications and manage device permissions.

We update this page when our practices change and explain material changes where appropriate.
