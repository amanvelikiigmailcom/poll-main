# Proposed additions to the published Flyprox Privacy Policy

Prepared for the local-game update on November 17, 2026. The published Notion policy has been read; it has not been edited by this change.

These additions describe implementation, not a certification of GDPR compliance. Keep the existing controller identity, support-provider disclosures and other policy sections. Before publication, verify the owner’s legal grounds, support retention, processor arrangements, international transfers, target age/countries and any applicable EU representation requirements.

## Section 2 — add to the on-device data list

- Your optional profile photo, saved as a small PNG image.
- Your choices and skipped questions in the current round and most recently completed round, including the question text and your local player label.
- Your sound, haptics and reduced-motion preferences.
- Participant names you type or pick from your contacts. Contacts are chosen in system UI; the app receives only the names you select and never reads your full address book. Names stay on this device.
- Your age-gate status (verified or blocked) and its check timestamp. The selected age itself is not retained, and nothing about the check leaves the device.

Reveal shows your own saved choices on this device. Flyprox does not receive votes from the people whose names appear on the cards. The latest completed round replaces the previously completed round; choices are not retained as a growing history of completed games.

## Section 3 — replace the first paragraph

If you choose a profile image, Flyprox uses the device image picker to access the image you select. The app saves a reduced-size PNG copy locally for your profile, without retaining the original image’s embedded metadata in that saved copy. This local profile flow does not upload the image to a Flyprox backend. You can replace your photo or choose Remove photo and Save in Edit profile. The original in your photo library is not changed. Temporary files created by the operating system or image picker may be subject to platform cache cleanup.

## Section 8 — replace the reset description

In Flyprox, open Profile → Settings → Reset game and confirm. This removes the stored local game profile, participant names, optional profile photo, current and last-round choices, university details, stars, question history, round progress and cooldown. Setup-completion and effect preferences are kept. Reset does not erase an export you copied, correspondence already sent to support, the original photo in your photo library or records held by another app. Device backups remain controlled through your device and platform settings.

## Section 10 — add

Settings → View my local data lets you inspect a JSON representation of your local profile, optional photo, current and last-round choices and game totals. Copy JSON copies this information to your clipboard only when you choose it. It can include private information; share it only with people you intend. Profile fields can be corrected through Edit profile. You can ask about information sent to support by contacting support@flyprox.com.

## Before making a GDPR compliance claim

Confirm the controller’s contact details, purposes and legal grounds per processing activity; the applicable rights and complaint route; actual support retention; providers’ roles, contracts and transfer safeguards; treatment of children; security and breach-response obligations; and whether an EU representative or other formal measures are required. Do not equate Apple’s “Data Not Collected” definition with an exemption from GDPR.
