# Proposed published-page corrections for build 14

These are factual product-description replacements for the two existing Flyprox
Notion pages. They are prepared for owner review; they have not been published.
Other page sections remain unchanged. This document does not assert legal approval.

## Privacy Policy

Page: https://distinct-lunaria-d0a.notion.site/41ae779d27428120b9bbfb769e7063f6

Update the effective date to November 25, 2026. Add to section 3:

> Build 14 adds campus-based online rounds, username search, friend requests and
> received Likes. Search and recommendation cards use usernames; confirmed friends
> and permitted profile views may show profile names. The backend stores online
> votes and their eligible candidate snapshots. Ordinary Likes, Activity and push
> notifications do not identify the voter.
>
> In eligible new online rounds, Premium recipients can spend limited weekly
> reveals to see the voter's first initial or profile first and last name. The app
> explains this before voting and before submitting the round. Only rounds created
> under the new reveal policy qualify; older votes do not become revealable. The
> backend checks subscription status, recipient ownership, account availability
> and blocks. It stores private reveal receipts and weekly usage; unlocked names
> are read from the profile and are not copied into the receipts. Online votes
> should not be treated as anonymous.

Replace the section 4 paragraph beginning "Selected names are used as labels":

> Selected names are used locally as labels on your quiz cards. In build 14, if
> signed in, the app sends hashes of selected phone numbers to match registered
> accounts; contact labels stay on the device. Older builds may have uploaded
> selected labels and existing matching records may retain them. Raw selected
> game-contact phone numbers and your full address book are not uploaded. Hashes
> can be matched to accounts and are not anonymous data.

Add to section 6:

> Premium Weekly provides up to two full-name voter reveals and five first-initial
> reveals per subscription week, double stars for completed online rounds and a
> membership badge. The badge denotes an active subscription, not identity
> verification. The backend uses verified subscription ownership, state and expiry
> to grant these benefits. An app or SDK purchase result alone does not grant
> server access. Cancellation preserves the paid period; expiry, refund or
> revocation removes access.

Replace the first paragraph of section 7:

> You choose whether to open an external app or send an invitation. Selecting a
> friend for an optional invitation sends only that selected number to the backend
> to create a keyed, account-scoped recipient hash; the number is not stored.
> Invitation links contain signed attribution information. A Messages sent result
> or the first five qualifying WhatsApp returns after more than four seconds per
> UTC day can grant one extra round per eligible recipient. Later WhatsApp grants
> require recipient confirmation through the link. A link preview or opening the
> share screen alone does not grant a round. These signals do not prove delivery
> or installation. External services process messages under their own policies.

## Terms of Use

Update the effective date to November 25, 2026. Append to section 3:

> In build 14, signed-in profile fields, selected campus and graduation year are
> managed on the server. Each saved change to first name, surname, username,
> campus or graduation year starts an independent 90-day editing wait. Gender is
> not editable in this flow. A campus request or skip does not by itself unlock
> school gameplay.

Replace the last paragraph of section 3 concerning matching:

> You are responsible for the participant labels you choose. Use the example path
> if you do not want to share contacts. Avoid sensitive information and do not
> misuse another person's information. In build 14, selected phone-number hashes
> are sent for signed-in account matching while contact labels stay local. Older
> builds may have sent selected labels. The full address book and raw selected
> game-contact numbers are not uploaded. Selecting a contact does not send an
> invitation. Optional invitations are described separately in our Privacy Policy.

Replace the section 5 rules after its first paragraph:

> A completed 12-question round earns 1,000 stars, or 2,000 for an online round
> with server-confirmed Premium. The break is 60 minutes for everyone, including
> Premium. There is a maximum of 50 completed online rounds per UTC day.
>
> Stars have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred for money.
> Collection offers server-confirmed boosts: 5,000 stars for placements in four
> questions of one eligible future player's school round, including at most one
> crush question; 20,000 stars for one crush-question placement. A placement does
> not guarantee a view or vote. Campus, self-voting and block restrictions apply.
> Purchases may wait in a queue; changing campus pauses unfulfilled rights until
> return to the original campus.
>
> Server-confirmed invitations can grant an extra round per eligible recipient:
> Messages marked sent or the first five qualifying WhatsApp returns in a UTC day
> can qualify, with recipient confirmation required for later WhatsApp grants.
> Link previews and opening a sharing screen alone do not qualify. Invitation
> rounds do not bypass the daily cap, and unused earned rounds remain saved.

In section 6 retain the existing Apple price, billing, cancellation, refund and
restore wording, and replace only the sentence describing Premium benefits:

> Premium Weekly includes two full-name voter reveals and five first-initial
> reveals per subscription week, double stars for completed online rounds and a
> blue membership badge while active. The two reveal limits reset independently
> in subscription-week windows, not calendar weeks, with no rollover. Reopening
> an already unlocked vote is free while Premium remains active. Only eligible
> new votes can be revealed; older votes remain hidden. The app explains voter
> disclosure before voting and submitting. Premium does not skip the 60-minute
> break. The old Full Reveal of your own answers is removed.
